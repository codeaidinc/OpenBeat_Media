## About Middle East Watch

**Middle East Watch** is a reference-media pilot that routinely monitors how developments in the Middle East (geopolitics) affect small and mid-sized businesses and their day-to-day operations. We draw on **public information** from governments, ministries, international organizations, major media and official statistics, and we **cite a source for every item**. The publication runs entirely on open-source collection, editing and distribution tools (OpenBeat).

Our focus areas are energy (crude oil, LNG, fuel), maritime logistics and shipping (Hormuz, the Red Sea, Suez), supply chains and procurement, and prices and inflation (cost pass-through, FX).

### Editorial policy

- We use **public information only** and handle no personal data.
- Every article cites its **sources (URLs)** — full provenance.
- We stay **neutral**, favor no country or side, and keep claims within what the sources support.
- We do not reproduce full text; we **summarize and link** to the original.
- Figures are as of each source's publication date. Prices and conditions change, so please check the latest at each source.
